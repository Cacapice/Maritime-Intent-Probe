# Enterprise Transformation Role

## Construct-Validity Engine — Phase 1

Tests whether semantic interpretation is identifiable. Phase 1 does not produce an operational intent monitor.

This repository is deployed as a service inside the **Sovereign Evidence Architecture**. It receives only the minimum data or local execution capability required for its estimand, emits qualified aggregate evidence, and never assumes ownership of source data or decision authority.

## Operating model

- **Owner:** defines purpose, jurisdiction, retention, and acceptable disclosure.
- **Custodian:** operates the data plane under the owner's policy.
- **Analytical service:** executes locally or in an approved enclave and emits a scientific result.
- **Privacy authority:** assesses disclosure and identity risk.
- **Publication authority:** approves external release after owner approval.
- **Decision owner:** interprets the qualified evidence and remains accountable for action.

## Transformation outcome

The enterprise moves from centralized data accumulation to governed computation: raw records remain sovereign; reusable analytical services travel to the data; only qualified, purpose-bound evidence crosses the trust boundary.

## Phase 1 constraint

The service is limited to construct-validity diagnosis and experiment redesign. It is not an operational monitoring service and cannot be represented as one by downstream policy.
