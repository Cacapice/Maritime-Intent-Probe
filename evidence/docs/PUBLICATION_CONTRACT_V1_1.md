# Publication Contract v1.1

This repository emits a versioned `ScientificResult`, evidence graph, provenance record, JSON Schemas, and SHA-256 manifest.

## Research phase

**Phase 1 construct-validity diagnostic.**

For Maritime, Phase 1 means the repository establishes and tests the construct-validity/identifiability gate and model-blind witness. It does **not** claim completion of the later crossed 2×2 intent/action-space validation, cross-architecture replication, or a deployable intent monitor.

## Integrity

Run `python verify_publication_bundle.py <bundle-directory>` to verify all declared hashes and the schema/phase relationship. Qualifications may preserve, weaken, or block a claim; absent new evidence, they may never increase epistemic strength.
