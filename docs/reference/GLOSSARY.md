# Glossary

Terms used in this repository, in plain language.

**Probe.** A small classifier trained on a language model's internal activity to
predict some property of the input.

**Construct validity.** Whether a measurement captures the thing it is meant to
capture, as opposed to something correlated with it.

**Identifiability.** Whether the data could distinguish 2 competing explanations
at all. If the study never varied them separately, no amount of data or model
capacity can separate them.

**Model-blind witness.** A statistic computed without running the model, such as
the number of conversational turns. If it matches the probe, the probe's accuracy
cannot be credited to anything the model contributed.

**Minimal pair.** Two inputs differing in a single controlled respect.

**Support.** The combinations of conditions the study actually observed. Cells
outside the support are not merely rare, they are absent.
